# Arabic word-sign dataset research

Reviewed 2026-10-21 from primary author/repository pages. **No third-party sign dataset was downloaded, used to fit weights, or recommended for MAEEN product training/distribution.** Owner confirmed no authorized corpus and directed first-party collection. A downloadable dataset or permissive paper/code license is not automatically a grant for its recordings, derived models or distribution.

## Letter-only dataset screened separately

[ArSL-31-Pilot](https://zenodo.org/records/18363162) declares CC BY 4.0 and 7,010 samples across 28 letters and three controls, with 89 features (42 xy coordinates plus engineered geometry). Signer separation is not established. It is **not a word dataset** and does not match MAEEN's 63 raw xyz/37-class forest. Filling absent z with zeros or pretending label equivalence would not be a scientific evaluation of the existing model. No download/use performed.

## Selection decision and ongoing eligibility checklist

Proceed with first-party Saudi recordings under the explicit consent/review protocol. A future public candidate is eligible only after verifying: exact version and authentic class manifest; per-clip signer/session IDs; temporal recordings with sufficient real repetitions; documented Saudi/regional compatibility; explicit intended training **and model distribution** permission; underlying media/participant rights; and feasible signer-disjoint splits. Written permission requests are prepared as an owner action; no email or third-party message was sent.

No public-paper accuracy, pretrained checkpoint score, image count or synthetic example is reported as MAEEN accuracy. No candidate's list is advertised in the app as supported vocabulary.
