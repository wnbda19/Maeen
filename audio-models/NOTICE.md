# Google YAMNet

Copyright Google LLC / TensorFlow contributors. Distributed under Apache License 2.0; see LICENSE.

Original pretrained model from the official MediaPipe audio-classifier download. Model licensing is declared by Google's [official YAMNet TFLite listing](https://www.kaggle.com/models/google/yamnet/tfLite). Source and exact hash are recorded in provenance.json. No MAEEN fine-tuning or weight modifications.

YAMNet scores are not calibrated probabilities. AudioSet class membership does not establish safety reliability. MAEEN exposes only the explicitly mapped classes and does not identify direction, distance, a specific vehicle, or the material of a shattering object.
